How to Calculate Your Wyoming Take-Home Pay

  1. Start with your gross annual salary.
  2. Subtract the 2026 federal standard deduction ($16,100 single) to get taxable income, then apply the 2026 federal brackets (10%-37%).
  3. Subtract FICA: 6.2% Social Security (up to the $184,500 wage base) + 1.45% Medicare (all wages, +0.9% above $200,000).
  4. Wyoming charges no state income tax — your paycheck stops there. What's left is your net take-home pay.

Example: $65,000/year gross salary, single filer, Wyoming resident.

  1. Federal tax: $5,620
  2. FICA: $4,972.5
  3. Wyoming state tax: No state income tax.
  4. Net annual pay: $65,000 - $10,592.5 = $54,407.5 (≈$4,533.96/month)

Wyoming Paycheck Calculator FAQ

Does Wyoming have a state income tax?

No. Wyoming is one of nine states with no personal income tax. Only federal income tax and FICA are withheld from your paycheck.

How much is take-home pay on $65,000 in Wyoming?

On a $65,000 gross salary, a single filer in Wyoming takes home roughly $54,407.5/year (about $4,533.96/month) after federal tax, FICA.
