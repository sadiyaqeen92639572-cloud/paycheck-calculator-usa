How to Calculate Your Florida Take-Home Pay

  1. Start with your gross annual salary.
  2. Subtract the 2026 federal standard deduction ($16,100 single) to get taxable income, then apply the 2026 federal brackets (10%-37%).
  3. Subtract FICA: 6.2% Social Security (up to the $184,500 wage base) + 1.45% Medicare (all wages, +0.9% above $200,000).
  4. Florida charges no state income tax — your paycheck stops there. What's left is your net take-home pay.

Example: $65,000/year gross salary, single filer, Florida resident.

  1. Federal taxable income: $65,000 - $16,100 = $48,900 → federal tax = $1,240 + ($48,900 - $12,400) × 12% = $5,620
  2. FICA: $65,000 × 7.65% = $4,972.50
  3. Florida state tax: $0
  4. Net annual pay: $65,000 - $5,620 - $4,972.50 = $54,407.50 (≈$4,533.96/month)

Florida Paycheck Calculator FAQ

Does Florida have a state income tax?

No. Florida's constitution (art. VII, § 5) has barred a state personal income tax since 1924. Only federal income tax and FICA are withheld from Florida paychecks.

How much is take-home pay on $65,000 in Florida?

On a $65,000 gross salary, a single filer in Florida takes home roughly $54,440/year (about $4,537/month) after federal income tax and FICA — no state tax is withheld.

Why does Florida have no income tax?

Florida funds state government primarily through sales tax and tourism-related revenue instead of a personal income tax, a structure protected by its state constitution.
