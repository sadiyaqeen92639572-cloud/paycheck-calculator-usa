How Your Paycheck Is Calculated

Federal income tax — the same 2026 IRS brackets (10%-37%) and $16,100 single-filer standard deduction apply no matter which state you're in.

FICA — 6.2% Social Security (up to the $184,500 wage base) + 1.45% Medicare (+0.9% above $200,000), also identical nationwide.

State income tax — this is where paychecks diverge. Texas and Florida charge $0. Pennsylvania, Illinois, Georgia, North Carolina, and Michigan use a flat rate. California, New York, and Ohio use progressive brackets. A few states (CA, NY) also add small mandatory payroll deductions like SDI or Paid Family Leave, and three (OH, PA, NY) have local/municipal income tax layers this calculator doesn't compute — each affected state page flags that explicitly.
