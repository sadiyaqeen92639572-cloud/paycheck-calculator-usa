How to Calculate Your Nebraska Take-Home Pay

  1. Start with your gross annual salary.
  2. Subtract the 2026 federal standard deduction ($16,100 single) to get taxable income, then apply the 2026 federal brackets (10%-37%).
  3. Subtract FICA: 6.2% Social Security (up to the $184,500 wage base) + 1.45% Medicare (all wages, +0.9% above $200,000).
  4. Subtract Nebraska's $16,100 standard deduction from gross wages, then apply the 3-bracket state schedule (2.46%-4.55%) marginally.
  5. What's left is your net take-home pay.

Example: $65,000/year gross salary, single filer, Nebraska resident.

  1. Federal tax: $5,620
  2. FICA: $4,972.5
  3. Nebraska state tax: Marginal tax on $48,900 taxable income (after $16,100 deduction) = $2,012.57
  4. Net annual pay: $65,000 - $12,605.07 = $52,394.93 (≈$4,366.24/month)

Nebraska Paycheck Calculator FAQ

What are Nebraska's income tax brackets for 2026?

Nebraska uses 3 marginal brackets ranging from 2.46% to 4.55%. Standard deduction: $16,100 for single filers.

How much is take-home pay on $65,000 in Nebraska?

On a $65,000 gross salary, a single filer in Nebraska takes home roughly $52,394.93/year (about $4,366.24/month) after federal tax, FICA, and state tax.
