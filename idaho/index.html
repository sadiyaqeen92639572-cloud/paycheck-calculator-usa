How to Calculate Your Idaho Take-Home Pay

  1. Start with your gross annual salary.
  2. Subtract the 2026 federal standard deduction ($16,100 single) to get taxable income, then apply the 2026 federal brackets (10%-37%).
  3. Subtract FICA: 6.2% Social Security (up to the $184,500 wage base) + 1.45% Medicare (all wages, +0.9% above $200,000).
  4. Subtract Idaho's $16,100 standard deduction from gross wages, then apply the flat 5.30% state rate.
  5. What's left is your net take-home pay.

Example: $65,000/year gross salary, single filer, Idaho resident.

  1. Federal tax: $5,620
  2. FICA: $4,972.5
  3. Idaho state tax: ($65,000 - $16,100 deduction) × 5.30% = $2,591.7
  4. Net annual pay: $65,000 - $13,184.2 = $51,815.8 (≈$4,317.98/month)

Idaho Paycheck Calculator FAQ

What is Idaho's state income tax rate?

Idaho charges a flat 5.30% rate for tax year 2026. Standard deduction: $16,100 for single filers.

How much is take-home pay on $65,000 in Idaho?

On a $65,000 gross salary, a single filer in Idaho takes home roughly $51,815.8/year (about $4,317.98/month) after federal tax, FICA, and state tax.
