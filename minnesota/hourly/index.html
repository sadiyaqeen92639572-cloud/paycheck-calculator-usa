Minnesota Hourly Wage Tax FAQ

What does $20 an hour come to after taxes in Minnesota?

About $34,000-$35,000 net per year for a single full-time worker (40 hours/week), after federal tax, 7.65% FICA and Minnesota state tax. Minnesota's income tax starts at 5.35%, so take-home is a little lower than in no-income-tax states.

How is overtime taxed in Minnesota?

At the same rates as regular hourly pay - Minnesota brackets (5.35%-9.85%), federal tax and 7.65% FICA. Time-and-a-half increases gross pay, which can push part of your income into a higher Minnesota or federal bracket.

Does Minnesota have any local income tax on wages?

No. Minnesota has no city or county income tax, so the hourly calculator only applies state plus federal plus FICA.
