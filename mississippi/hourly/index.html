How to Calculate Your Mississippi Take-Home Pay

  1. Start with your gross annual salary.
  2. Subtract the 2026 federal standard deduction ($16,100 single) to get taxable income, then apply the 2026 federal brackets (10%-37%).
  3. Subtract FICA: 6.2% Social Security (up to the $184,500 wage base) + 1.45% Medicare (all wages, +0.9% above $200,000).
  4. Subtract Mississippi's $10,000 standard deduction from gross wages, then apply the flat 4.40% state rate.
  5. What's left is your net take-home pay.

Example: $65,000/year gross salary, single filer, Mississippi resident.

  1. Federal tax: $5,620
  2. FICA: $4,972.5
  3. Mississippi state tax: ($65,000 - $10,000 deduction) × 4.00% = $2,200
  4. Net annual pay: $65,000 - $12,792.5 = $52,207.5 (≈$4,350.63/month)

Mississippi Paycheck Calculator FAQ

What is Mississippi's state income tax rate?

Mississippi charges a flat 4.40% rate for tax year 2026. Standard deduction: $10,000 for single filers.

How much is take-home pay on $65,000 in Mississippi?

On a $65,000 gross salary, a single filer in Mississippi takes home roughly $51,987.5/year (about $4,332.29/month) after federal tax, FICA, and state tax.

What deductions does Mississippi apply before taxing my income?

Mississippi applies a $10,000 standard deduction, before the flat 4.40% rate. See the "How Mississippi Paycheck Tax Is Calculated" section above for the exact formula.

Does this estimate change if I'm married or have dependents?

This calculator uses single-filer figures only (v1 scope). Married filing jointly typically uses different federal and Mississippi state brackets and a larger standard deduction — your actual take-home pay will differ. Dependents can also reduce federal withholding via the W-4. Check Mississippi Department of Revenue's married-filing-jointly tables for a precise figure.
