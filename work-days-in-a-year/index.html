How Many Work Days Are in a Year?

2026: 250 work days · 2027: 249 work days · U.S. federal holidays

Reference information, not legal or payroll advice. Counts use Monday-to-Friday weeks and the U.S. federal holiday calendar published by the Office of Personnel Management. Private employers choose their own paid holidays, so your workplace may differ.

The short answer

2026 has 250 work days: 261 weekdays minus 11 federal holidays. 2027 has 249: 261 weekdays minus 12 holidays. Ignoring holidays, a standard year has 261 weekdays (260 or 261, depending on the calendar).

Federal holidays and when they are observed

When a federal holiday falls on a Saturday, the Friday before is the observed holiday; when it falls on a Sunday, the Monday after is observed.

2,080 hours or 2,000 hours? Paid vs worked

2,080 is paid hours (52 weeks × 40), and holidays are inside that number. It is the figure to use when turning an hourly rate into a salary: $25/hr × 2,080 = $52,000. About 2,000 is hours worked once the roughly 11 holidays come out (250 × 8 = 2,000 in 2026). Take another 10 vacation days and you are at 240 days, or 1,920 hours worked.

Work Days in a Year FAQ

How many work days are in a year?

A normal year has 365 days and 52 weeks plus one day, which gives 261 weekdays (Monday to Friday) in 2026 and 261 in 2027. Counting out the U.S. federal holidays, that is 250 work days in 2026 and 249 in 2027. If your employer pays for holidays you can still call it about 260 paid days.

How many work days are in 2026?

2026 has 261 weekdays and 11 federal holidays that land on a weekday, so 250 work days, or 2,000 hours at 8 hours a day.

How many work days are in 2027?

2027 has 261 weekdays and 12 observed federal holidays, so 249 work days (1,992 hours at 8 hours a day). 2027 has one more observed holiday than 2026 because New Year's Day 2028 falls on a Saturday, so the federal holiday is observed on Friday, December 31, 2027.

How many working hours are in a year?

A full-time salary is usually figured on 2,080 paid hours (52 weeks × 40 hours), which includes paid holidays. Hours actually worked are lower: 250 work days × 8 = 2,000 in 2026, and 1,992 in 2027, before any vacation or sick leave.

Is a work year 2,080 hours or 2,000 hours?

Both, for different questions. 2,080 is paid hours (52 × 40) and is what payroll uses to turn an hourly rate into an annual salary. About 2,000 is hours worked once you take out roughly 10 paid holidays. Use 2,080 to convert pay, and use the work-day count here to plan project time or count billable days.

How many work days are in a month?

On average 20.8 work days in 2026 (250 ÷ 12). A single month ranges from about 19 to 23 depending on how the weekends and holidays fall; the month-by-month table above lists each one.

Do private employers have to follow the federal holidays?

No. Federal law does not require private employers to give any paid holidays. The federal list is the common baseline, but many private employers observe fewer (often 6 to 10) and some use different observed dates, so check your own handbook. Using the federal calendar here is a standard, sourced reference point, not a rule for every workplace.

How many work days are in a leap year?

A leap year has 366 days, but the extra day only adds a work day when it lands on a weekday. 2028 is a leap year that starts on a Saturday, so it has 260 weekdays and 250 work days after 10 federal holidays.

Methodology & Source

Weekdays are counted from the calendar. Holidays follow the U.S. federal holiday schedule from the Office of Personnel Management (opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/pay-leave/federal-holidays/) under 5 U.S.C. 6103, with Saturday holidays observed on the preceding Friday and Sunday holidays on the following Monday (Executive Order 11582). A holiday is counted in the year of its observed date, so New Year's Day 2028 counts toward 2027 when it is observed on December 31. Verified 2026-10-01.
