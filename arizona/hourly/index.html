How to Calculate Your Arizona Take-Home Pay

  1. Start with your gross annual salary.
  2. Subtract the 2026 federal standard deduction ($16,100 single) to get taxable income, then apply the 2026 federal brackets (10%-37%).
  3. Subtract FICA: 6.2% Social Security (up to the $184,500 wage base) + 1.45% Medicare (all wages, +0.9% above $200,000).
  4. Subtract Arizona's $16,100 standard deduction from gross wages, then apply the flat 2.50% state rate.
  5. What's left is your net take-home pay.

Example: $65,000/year gross salary, single filer, Arizona resident.

  1. Federal tax: $5,620
  2. FICA: $4,972.5
  3. Arizona state tax: ($65,000 - $16,100 deduction) × 2.50% = $1,222.5
  4. Net annual pay: $65,000 - $11,815 = $53,185 (≈$4,432.08/month)

Arizona Paycheck Calculator FAQ

What is Arizona's state income tax rate?

Arizona charges a flat 2.50% rate for tax year 2026. Standard deduction: $16,100 for single filers.

How much is take-home pay on $65,000 in Arizona?

On a $65,000 gross salary, a single filer in Arizona takes home roughly $53,185/year (about $4,432.08/month) after federal tax, FICA, and state tax.

What deductions does Arizona apply before taxing my income?

Arizona applies a $16,100 standard deduction, before the flat 2.50% rate. See the "How Arizona Paycheck Tax Is Calculated" section above for the exact formula.

Does this estimate change if I'm married or have dependents?

This calculator uses single-filer figures only (v1 scope). Married filing jointly typically uses different federal and Arizona state brackets and a larger standard deduction — your actual take-home pay will differ. Dependents can also reduce federal withholding via the W-4. Check Arizona Department of Revenue's married-filing-jointly tables for a precise figure.

Methodology & Source

Arizona state tax figures sourced from Arizona Department of Revenue (https://azdor.gov/), citing Ariz. Rev. Stat. § 43-1011 — flat 2.5% rate effective 2023. Arizona's married-filing-jointly and head-of-household figures are not yet independently verified and currently fall back to single-filer brackets — see the caveat in the formula section above.

This is an estimate based on standard single-filer federal, FICA, and AZ state calculations. Actual withholding may differ based on your W-4 elections, pre-tax deductions, and other factors. Arizona conforms closely to the federal standard deduction; this estimate applies it directly.
