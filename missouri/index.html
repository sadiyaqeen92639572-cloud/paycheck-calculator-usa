How to Calculate Your Missouri Take-Home Pay

  1. Start with your gross annual salary.
  2. Subtract the 2026 federal standard deduction ($16,100 single) to get taxable income, then apply the 2026 federal brackets (10%-37%).
  3. Subtract FICA: 6.2% Social Security (up to the $184,500 wage base) + 1.45% Medicare (all wages, +0.9% above $200,000).
  4. Subtract Missouri's $15,750 standard deduction from gross wages, then apply the 8-bracket state schedule (0%-4.70%) marginally.
  5. What's left is your net take-home pay before any local tax.

Example: $65,000/year gross salary, single filer, Missouri resident.

  1. Federal tax: $5,620
  2. FICA: $4,972.5
  3. Missouri state tax: Marginal tax on $49,250 taxable income (after $15,750 deduction) = $2,138.81
  4. Net annual pay: $65,000 - $12,731.31 = $52,268.69 (≈$4,355.72/month)

Missouri Paycheck Calculator FAQ

What are Missouri's income tax brackets for 2026?

Missouri uses 8 marginal brackets ranging from 0% to 4.70%. Standard deduction: $15,750 for single filers.

How much is take-home pay on $65,000 in Missouri?

On a $65,000 gross salary, a single filer in Missouri takes home roughly $52,268.69/year (about $4,355.72/month) after federal tax, FICA, and state tax — before any local tax.

Does this calculator include Missouri local or city income tax?

Kansas City and St. Louis each levy a 1% local earnings tax on residents (and on nonresidents working there), not computed here.
