How to Calculate Your Pennsylvania Take-Home Pay

  1. Start with your gross annual salary.
  2. Subtract the 2026 federal standard deduction ($16,100 single), apply the 2026 federal brackets (10%-37%).
  3. Subtract FICA: 6.2% Social Security (up to $184,500) + 1.45% Medicare (+0.9% above $200,000).
  4. Subtract Pennsylvania's flat 3.07% state tax — applied to gross wages, with no standard deduction or personal exemption.
  5. What's left is your net take-home pay before any local Earned Income Tax (EIT).

Example: $65,000/year gross salary, single filer, Pennsylvania resident.

  1. Federal tax: $5,620
  2. FICA: $65,000 × 7.65% = $4,972.50
  3. PA state tax: $65,000 × 3.07% = $1,995.50
  4. Net annual pay: $65,000 - $5,620 - $4,972.50 - $1,995.50 = $52,412 (≈$4,367.67/month, before local EIT)

Pennsylvania Paycheck Calculator FAQ

What is Pennsylvania's state income tax rate?

Pennsylvania charges a flat 3.07% Personal Income Tax on gross wages, a rate that has not changed since 2004. There is no standard deduction or personal exemption.

Does this calculator include Philadelphia or other local wage taxes?

Philadelphia is now supported — select Philadelphia resident or non-resident from the local tax dropdown (3.735% resident / 3.425% non-resident, effective 2026-07-01). Every other Pennsylvania municipality and school district still levies its own separate Earned Income Tax (EIT), commonly 1%-3.9%+, which this calculator does not compute.

How much is take-home pay on $65,000 in Pennsylvania?

On a $65,000 gross salary, a single filer in Pennsylvania takes home roughly $52,412/year (about $4,370/month) after federal tax, FICA, and the state's 3.07% flat tax — before any local EIT.
