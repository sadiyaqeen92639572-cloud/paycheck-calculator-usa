How the Philadelphia Wage Tax Works (2026)

The Wage Tax is a flat percentage of gross wages — there is no standard deduction or bracket. Residents pay 3.735% on all wages regardless of where the work is done. Non-residents pay 3.425% on wages for work physically performed in Philadelphia. It is withheld by your employer alongside federal, FICA and Pennsylvania state tax.

Philadelphia Wage Tax FAQ

What is the Philadelphia Wage Tax rate for 2026?

The Philadelphia City Wage Tax is 3.735% for residents (on all wages, wherever earned) and 3.425% for non-residents on wages earned from work performed in Philadelphia, effective 2026-07-01 as part of a phased reduction. It applies to gross wages with no deductions.

Do non-residents pay Philadelphia Wage Tax?

Yes - non-residents pay 3.425% on the portion of wages earned working inside Philadelphia. Employers withhold it automatically; if you work partly outside the city you may be able to file for a partial refund with the Department of Revenue.

Is the Philadelphia Wage Tax on top of Pennsylvania state tax?

Yes. Pennsylvania's flat 3.07% Personal Income Tax applies statewide; the Philadelphia Wage Tax is a separate city tax on top of it. Total state + city income tax for a Philadelphia resident is about 6.80%.

Does the Philadelphia Wage Tax apply to a bonus?

Yes - the Wage Tax applies to bonuses and other supplemental wages at the same rate as regular pay. Use the Pennsylvania bonus tax calculator for a bonus estimate.
