Oregon Paycheck Calculator FAQ

What are Oregon's income tax brackets for 2026?

Oregon uses 4 marginal brackets ranging from 4.75% to 9.90%. Standard deduction: $2,875 for single filers.

How much is take-home pay on $65,000 in Oregon?

On a $65,000 gross salary, a single filer in Oregon takes home roughly $49,256.56/year (about $4,104.71/month) after federal tax, FICA, and state tax.

What deductions does Oregon apply before taxing my income?

Oregon applies a $2,875 standard deduction, before its marginal bracket schedule. See the "How Oregon Paycheck Tax Is Calculated" section above for the exact formula.

Does this estimate change if I'm married or have dependents?

This calculator uses single-filer figures only (v1 scope). Married filing jointly typically uses different federal and Oregon state brackets and a larger standard deduction — your actual take-home pay will differ. Dependents can also reduce federal withholding via the W-4. Check Oregon Department of Revenue's married-filing-jointly tables for a precise figure.

Methodology & Source

Oregon state tax figures sourced from Oregon Department of Revenue (https://www.oregon.gov/dor/), citing Or. Rev. Stat. § 316.037. Federal brackets, standard deductions (single/MFJ/HoH), and FICA constants sourced from the IRS (Revenue Procedure 2025-32; married-filing-jointly Additional Medicare threshold of $250,000 is a separate, unindexed statutory figure). Oregon's married-filing-jointly and head-of-household figures are not yet independently verified and currently fall back to single-filer brackets — see the caveat in the formula section above.

Bonus federal withholding uses the flat supplemental-wage rate (22%, 37% above $1,000,000 cumulative supplemental wages/year), per IRS Publication 15 (Circular E), Employer's Tax Guide, 2026 edition — the alternative "aggregate method" is not modeled.

This is an estimate based on standard single-filer federal, FICA, and OR state calculations. Actual withholding may differ based on your W-4 elections, pre-tax deductions, and other factors.
