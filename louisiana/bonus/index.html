Louisiana Paycheck Calculator FAQ

What is Louisiana's state income tax rate?

Louisiana charges a flat 3% rate for tax year 2026. Standard deduction: $12,500 for single filers.

How much is take-home pay on $65,000 in Louisiana?

On a $65,000 gross salary, a single filer in Louisiana takes home roughly $52,832.5/year (about $4,402.71/month) after federal tax, FICA, and state tax.

What deductions does Louisiana apply before taxing my income?

Louisiana applies a $12,500 standard deduction, before the flat 3% rate. See the "How Louisiana Paycheck Tax Is Calculated" section above for the exact formula.

Does this estimate change if I'm married or have dependents?

This calculator uses single-filer figures only (v1 scope). Married filing jointly typically uses different federal and Louisiana state brackets and a larger standard deduction — your actual take-home pay will differ. Dependents can also reduce federal withholding via the W-4. Check Louisiana Department of Revenue's married-filing-jointly tables for a precise figure.

Methodology & Source

Louisiana state tax figures sourced from Louisiana Department of Revenue (https://revenue.louisiana.gov/), citing Constitutional Amendment 2 (Nov. 2024) — flat 3% rate replacing the prior 1.85%-4.25% brackets. Federal brackets, standard deductions (single/MFJ/HoH), and FICA constants sourced from the IRS (Revenue Procedure 2025-32; married-filing-jointly Additional Medicare threshold of $250,000 is a separate, unindexed statutory figure). Louisiana's married-filing-jointly and head-of-household figures are not yet independently verified and currently fall back to single-filer brackets — see the caveat in the formula section above.

This is an estimate based on standard single-filer federal, FICA, and LA state calculations. Actual withholding may differ based on your W-4 elections, pre-tax deductions, and other factors.
